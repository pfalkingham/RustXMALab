Qt 6 (qtbase): copyright and licence
====================================

Code ported from Qt 6 qtbase, so that files are read and written as the C++ XMALab
(built with Qt 6) reads and writes them:

- crates/xma-io/src/cparse.rs: colour names and #RRGGBB strings as QColor::fromString
  parses them (src/gui/painting/qcolor.cpp).
- crates/xma-io/src/export/mod.rs: QFileSystemEntry::completeBaseName
  (src/corelib/io/qfilesystementry.cpp).
- crates/xma-io/src/xml.rs: the XML writer's escaping and layout, from
  QXmlStreamWriterPrivate (src/corelib/serialization/qxmlstream.cpp).
