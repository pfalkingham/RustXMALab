Butterworth filter coefficients (liir): copyright and licence
=============================================================

Code ported from: XMALab's processing/ButterworthLowPassFilter.cpp, into
crates/xma-vision/src/filter.rs. XMALab's file takes the Butterworth low-pass
coefficients (dcof_bwlp, ccof_bwlp, sf_bwlp, binomial_mult) from liir.c of Exstrom
Laboratories; its header, reproduced below, gives the source and the licence.

liir: Copyright (C) 2007 Exstrom Laboratories LLC. Licence: GPL-2.0-or-later.
RustXMALab uses it under version 3 of the GPL (LICENSE at the top of this archive).

The same file of XMALab also holds a filtfilt function, which its header credits to a
Stack Overflow answer. That function is not ported: RustXMALab's zero-phase filtering is
its own code, written from a mathematical specification (see NOTICE.md). The header's
last lines, which name that answer, are therefore left out below.

The header of XMALab's processing/ButterworthLowPassFilter.cpp, up to those lines:
