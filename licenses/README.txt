Licence texts and notices of the works ported into RustXMALab
=============================================================

RustXMALab is distributed under GPL-3.0-only; its text is LICENSE at the top of this
archive. Parts of it are ported from, or copied from, the works below; NOTICE.md says
which parts. This folder holds their copyright notices and licence texts. The licences of
the Rust crates the programs are built from are in THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt.

Files marked "verbatim" are unchanged copies of the file named; the NOTICE files were
written for this archive and quote the source's own copyright and licence header verbatim.

    XMALab (XMA Lab), copyright 2015 Brown University: its license.txt, verbatim except
    converted from Windows-1252 to UTF-8. Applies to the whole port, to the icons and
    style sheet copied from XMALab, and to the interface ported from its forms.
