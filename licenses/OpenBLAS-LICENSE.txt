RustXMALab contains no OpenBLAS code. Its levmar port (crates/xma-vision/src/levmar.rs)
reproduces the results that the BLAS routines IDAMAX, DSYR, DGER and DGEMV give as
OpenBLAS 0.3.29 computes them, and the LAPACK routines DSYTF2 and DSYTRS (see
LAPACK-LICENSE.txt), because the C++ XMALab's levmar calls them through OpenBLAS.

The upstream OpenBLAS LICENSE file was not available on the machine this archive was made
on. The copyright lines and licence below are the "Files: *" section of the Debian
packaging's copyright file for OpenBLAS 0.3.29 (libopenblas0-pthread,
share/doc/libopenblas0-pthread/copyright), with Debian's formatting removed (the leading
space of each line, and "." for a blank line) and its "Copyright:" field written as one
"Copyright (c)" line per holder. Upstream: https://github.com/OpenMathLib/OpenBLAS
